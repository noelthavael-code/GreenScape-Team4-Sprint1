Meet Our Specialists

Isadore Bell

Climatology and Maintenance Specialist

With nearly 30 years of service, Isadore is one of our most experienced members, specializing in meticulous year-round maintenance and lawn care excellence.

  • Expert Climatology: Unparalleled understanding of how Central Ohio weather patterns and microclimates impact plant health.
  • Proactive Care: Adjusts schedules for everything from summer droughts to harsh winter freezes to prevent problems before they arise.
  • Comprehensive Maintenance: Manages aeration, dethatching, and seasonal clean-ups for a flawless year-round look.

Chris P. Bacon

Soil Science Specialist

For over 20 years, Chris has served as our authority on lawn terraforming and soil science, building healthy landscapes from the ground up.

  • Soil Manipulation: Expert in assessing soil structure, correcting grading issues, and improving drainage.
  • Terraforming: Essential for clients requiring radical changes to property topography or functionality.
  • Scientific Rigor: Uses meticulous analysis to diagnose pH imbalances and nutrient deficiencies at the root cause.

April May

Horticultural Expert

Bringing 14 years of experience, April specializes in flower bed design and regional flora to ensure your property remains vibrant year-round.

  • Bespoke Design: Crafts flower beds tailored to homeowner aesthetics, sunlight conditions, and maintenance goals.
  • Seasonal Vitality: Oversees specialized pruning, fertilization, and seasonal rotations to keep gardens flourishing.
  • Sustainable Growth: Focuses on ecologically sound practices and plant science to build stunning, long-lasting spaces.

Phil Dirt

Turf Health Specialist

Phil provides a scientifically tailored approach to nutrient management and pest control to cultivate emerald-green turf.

  • Nutrient Management: Designs seasonal fertilization programs adapted to specific Central Ohio soil types and grass varieties.
  • Pest & Disease Defense: Highly skilled in identifying and mitigating invasive weeds, grubs, and lawn diseases.
  • Responsible Care: Utilizes targeted, professional-grade treatments to protect your lawn while minimizing environmental impact.
